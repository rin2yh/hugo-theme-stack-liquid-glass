Welcome to **Stack Liquid Glass**, a glassmorphism-flavored Hugo theme derived from [Hugo Theme Stack](https://github.com/CaiJimmy/hugo-theme-stack). This demo post is here so you can see how a typical article looks once rendered.

The defining visual idea is *liquid glass*: layered translucent surfaces with subtle blurs, soft borders, and gentle gradients. Surfaces lift above the background and respond to hover with quiet ripples — the effect is meant to feel calm rather than flashy.

The theme ships with both a light and a dark palette. Switch between them from the toggle in the sidebar; your preference is stored locally and re-applied on the next visit. On first load the theme follows your operating system's color scheme.

Search is powered by Hugo's `/index.json` output. Every page is indexed at build time and queried on the client, so there is no external service to configure — just type into the search box on the homepage and start filtering.
