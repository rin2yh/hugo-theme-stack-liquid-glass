This is the demo site for the **Stack Liquid Glass** Hugo theme. It exists to showcase the theme's layout, widgets, and content types — not as a personal blog.

The theme is a glassmorphism-flavored fork of [Hugo Theme Stack](https://github.com/CaiJimmy/hugo-theme-stack). Browse the posts to see how articles, external link entries, table of contents, and Mermaid diagrams render in context.
